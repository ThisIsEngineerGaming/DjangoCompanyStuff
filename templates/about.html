About the Company

Our story

Slippery Company was founded in 2010 by a small team dreaming of the perfect house slipper. From a tiny workshop we grew into a company with offices in Kyiv and Odesa.

Mission

To make everyday comfort accessible to everyone — from the first step in the morning to the last one in the evening.

Values

  • Comfort without compromise
  • High-quality, eco-friendly materials
  • Honesty with customers and partners
  • Safety in every step — a sole that doesn't slip!
